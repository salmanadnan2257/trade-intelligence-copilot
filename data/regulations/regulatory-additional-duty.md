# Regulatory duty and additional customs duty

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. What they are
Beyond basic customs duty, Pakistan can apply a regulatory duty and an additional customs duty on selected imports. Regulatory duty is often used as a policy lever, for example to discourage imports of certain goods or to protect local industry, and it can be introduced or changed by notification.

## 2. Why it matters for planning
Because these duties are adjusted relatively often, the total landed cost of an import can move without any change in the basic tariff. An importer planning a shipment should check whether a regulatory duty or additional customs duty currently applies to the specific HS line.

## 3. Not a fixed number
This summary does not state any specific regulatory-duty percentage, because such rates change by notification. Confirm the current figure from the latest official schedule before pricing a deal.
