# Molasses (HS 1703)

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. What sits in heading 1703
HS heading 1703 covers molasses resulting from the extraction or refining of sugar, including cane molasses. Molasses is a by-product of sugar production and is traded as an input for animal feed, fermentation, alcohol and yeast production, and some food uses.

## 2. Trade notes
Molasses is a bulk, low-unit-value commodity compared with refined sugar, so logistics and storage matter. As with other chapter 17 goods, molasses trade can be affected by domestic supply policy and by any export-management measures in place at a given time.

## 3. Confirming requirements
For a specific molasses shipment, confirm the applicable tariff line, any quality or food-safety conditions if used for human consumption, and the current policy status. This summary does not state a specific rate.
