# Certificate of origin and preferential trade

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. What origin means for duty
The country of origin of goods can change the duty rate when a trade agreement offers preferential (lower) tariffs. To claim a preference, an importer usually presents a certificate of origin proving the goods qualify under the agreement's origin rules.

## 2. Free trade and preferential agreements
Pakistan has preferential and free-trade arrangements with several partners. Goods that meet the origin rules of the relevant agreement can enter at reduced or zero duty, while goods from other origins pay the standard rate. The rules of origin, not just where a ship sailed from, decide eligibility.

## 3. Getting it right
A missing or incorrect certificate of origin means the preference is denied and full duty applies. Importers confirm both that an agreement covers the product and that the goods actually satisfy its origin rules before claiming a lower rate.
