# Sales tax and withholding on imports

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. Taxes at import
In Pakistan, imports are generally subject to sales tax at the import stage and to a withholding of income tax collected at import, in addition to customs duties. These are collected by customs at clearance and later adjusted in the importer's tax filings.

## 2. Registered versus unregistered
The applicable rates and the ability to reclaim input tax often depend on whether the importer is registered for sales tax and on the nature of the goods, for example raw material versus finished product. Registration status can change the effective cost.

## 3. Confirm the current rate
Standard rates and any exemptions change with each finance act and by notification, so this summary does not quote a percentage. Verify the current sales-tax and withholding treatment for the specific goods before import.
