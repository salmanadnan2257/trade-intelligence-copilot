# Anti-dumping and safeguard duties

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. Trade-remedy duties
Separate from ordinary customs duty, a country can impose trade-remedy measures. An anti-dumping duty targets goods sold for export at less than their normal value where this injures a domestic industry. A safeguard measure is a temporary restraint used when a surge of imports threatens serious injury. In Pakistan these are handled by the national tariff authority.

## 2. How they arise
These duties follow an investigation triggered by a complaint from a domestic industry. If the investigation confirms dumping or injurious import surge, a duty can be applied to specific products from specific origins for a defined period.

## 3. Effect on an importer
An anti-dumping or safeguard duty can substantially raise the landed cost of an affected product from an affected country, even when the basic tariff is unchanged. Importers check whether any trade-remedy duty currently applies to their product and origin.
