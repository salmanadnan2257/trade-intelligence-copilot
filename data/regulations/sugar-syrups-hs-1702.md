# Other sugars and syrups (HS 1702)

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. What sits in heading 1702
HS heading 1702 covers sugars other than the cane and beet sugar of 1701, plus sugar syrups. It includes glucose and glucose syrup, fructose and fructose syrup, lactose, maltose, maltodextrin, caramel, and invert sugar syrups. These are common food-industry inputs.

## 2. Why industry imports these
Products such as glucose syrup, high-fructose corn syrup, lactose, and maltodextrin are used by food, confectionery, dairy, and pharmaceutical manufacturers. They are often imported when local production does not cover industrial demand. In this dataset, lactose, dextrose, and glucose syrups are among the most frequently imported subcategories.

## 3. Classification care
Within 1702 the exact subheading depends on the sugar type and its composition, for example the fructose content of a syrup. Because duty and any regulatory conditions attach to the specific line, confirm the precise subheading for the actual product with the current tariff.
