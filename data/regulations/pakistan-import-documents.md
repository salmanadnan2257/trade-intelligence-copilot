# Core documents for importing goods into Pakistan

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. The usual paperwork
A commercial import into Pakistan typically needs a commercial invoice, a packing list, a bill of lading or airway bill, and a goods declaration filed electronically. Agricultural and food consignments often also need a phytosanitary or health certificate from the exporting country and clearance from the relevant Pakistani authority.

## 2. Filing the declaration
Declarations are filed through the Pakistan Single Window and the customs clearance system. The declarant selects the HS code, declares value and quantity, and pays the assessed duties and taxes before goods are released. Accurate HS codes and values are the declarant's responsibility.

## 3. Traders and agents
Many importers use a clearing agent and a shipping agent to handle filing, port handling, and delivery. In trade records you will often see the buyer (importer), the seller (foreign supplier), and a shipping or clearing agent named separately. Exact document lists vary by commodity, so confirm requirements with the current Import Policy Order and the clearing agent.
