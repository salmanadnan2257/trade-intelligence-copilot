# Sugar confectionery (HS 1704)

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. What sits in heading 1704
HS heading 1704 covers sugar confectionery not containing cocoa, including chewing gum, and certain white-chocolate style preparations classified here rather than under cocoa. In this dataset, heading 1704 subcategories include chewing gum and other sugar confectionery, and it is one of the more frequently occurring headings.

## 2. Food-safety angle
Finished confectionery is a consumer food product, so labeling, ingredient, and food-safety rules apply in addition to customs classification. Importers should expect food-standard checks alongside normal customs assessment.

## 3. Classification care
Whether a sweet product falls in 1704 or in a cocoa heading depends on its composition, especially cocoa content. Confirm the correct heading for the actual recipe with the current tariff before declaring.
