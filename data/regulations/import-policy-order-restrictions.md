# Import Policy Order and restricted or banned items

_Authored explanatory summary for demo purposes. Not legal advice._

## 1. The policy framework
Pakistan's Import Policy Order sets out what may be imported, what is banned, and what is allowed only under conditions such as prior permission, specific origin rules, or compliance certificates. It is updated from time to time, and a separate Export Policy Order governs exports.

## 2. Conditional and prohibited goods
Some goods are prohibited outright, often for security, health, religious, or environmental reasons. Others are conditional: allowed only with a permit, a specific certificate, or from approved sources. Agricultural goods can carry SPS and quality conditions in addition to any commercial restriction.

## 3. Check before shipping
Because the policy order changes, the safe practice is to confirm the current status of the exact goods, and any conditions, before contracting. This summary does not list current bans, which are subject to change by notification.
